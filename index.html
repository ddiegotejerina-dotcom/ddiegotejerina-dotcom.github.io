● In developmentOwn project · 2026

Geopolitical conflict monitor

Builds on my background in policy analysis and OSINT.

Problem
Following conflicts means reading dozens of sources of uneven quality, with no quick way to tell confirmed facts from noise.
What I'm building
A site that monitors curated sources twice a day, summarises what changed and rates each item by source reliability, with human review before publishing. Bilingual (ES/EN).
Scope
MVP: Russia–Ukraine, Latin America, Middle East and Africa. Sources grouped by power factor: economy, politics, military, science, society.
